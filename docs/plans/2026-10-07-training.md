# Public model-evaluation methodology

Updated 7 October 2026. Private training choices, target-specific adaptation plans, compute decisions and operational run details live in [Scrolls-private](https://github.com/ChaseHendrick/Scrolls-private) (access required). This page retains the public evaluation principles and source-backed research context. It does not report newly trained weights.

## Labelled controls

PHerc0139 w035 is a training segment and checks pipeline reproduction only. w045 is held out from released `ink_9um` and d9v2, but belongs to their training scroll; Reader v2 trained on w045 and cannot use it as a held-out test. PHerc0841 supplies labelled cross-scroll benchmarks for those released readers. Refer to each checkpoint's training inventory before claiming held-out status.

PHerc0841 w00 and ag896 trace the same papyrus sheet, so they are correlated observations rather than independent sheet replications. ag405 is a different surface. See [overlap measurements](../logs/2026-10-07-overlap-and-baseline.md). Preserve per-segment device, checkpoint revision, crop/full-map scope, coverage and depth settings in [results.json](../results.json); published community rows and our runs are distinct records.

For a new model-selection experiment, freeze the training exclusions, variants, selection rule and independent audit before fitting. Choices may use one PHerc0841 sheet; an untouched different sheet can audit the selected model once. Existing exploratory use of ag405 means it cannot retrospectively become an untouched test for those choices. Repeated development ultimately needs additional independent sheets or scrolls. Count and report every tried variant, and retain the audit result even if it fails. A mean over w00/ag896 does not provide two independent selection surfaces.

Pixel AUC against human ink labels inside supervision measures ranking, not legibility. Match forward, reversed and shuffled inputs, model settings, crops and masks. Report their scores separately; subtracting reverse AUC from forward AUC is not corrected accuracy. Letter-scale high-pass and rolled-label controls can expose nuisance correlation but do not establish ink identity. Selection must not tune geometry, thresholds or registration against audit labels.

## Public sources and testable ideas

- Youssef Nader's released [v8in](https://huggingface.co/YoussefMoNader/ink-8um-v8in), [PHerc1447 fine-tune](https://huggingface.co/YoussefMoNader/ink-8um-v8in-pherc1447-loo-w062) and public surfaces support a training-domain comparison. A released fine-tune can be evaluated before further training; saved partial comparisons are in the [cloud log](../logs/2026-10-07-cloud-partial.md). Score coverage and matched controls, rather than treating an interrupted run as complete.
- Public 1.1 um predictions may serve as a higher-resolution teacher than 2.4 um pseudo-labels, but alignment, label provenance and held-out exclusion must be established first. Greater nominal resolution is not proof of better supervision. The organizers' public `distilled_3d_ink` work is related prior art.
- Published scroll-specific pseudo-labelling and masked reconstruction motivate adaptation experiments, but their effect on independent label ranking and controls must be tested. Pseudo-labels can reinforce artifacts. See the [literature review](../logs/2026-10-07-literature.md), including [arXiv:2606.29085](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.29085) and [arXiv:2603.27698](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.27698).

Community model-card numbers and their settings are in the [community scan](../logs/2026-10-07-community-scan.md); they must not become a promotion bar without matching the actual scoring protocol. A model's domain, segmentation, inference depth and scoring-grid differences can change those numbers.

Dataset and checkpoint licences must be checked for the specific sources used. No data, maps or weights belong in Git. Private training plans and unpublished submission material remain in the private repository; generic evaluation tools and public-data reports remain here.
