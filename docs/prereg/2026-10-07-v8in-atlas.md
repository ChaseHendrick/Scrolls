# Preregistration: v8in on the public automatic meshes of PHerc0813, 0358 and 0826

Written 2026-10-07, before any v8in map of these surfaces exists. The commit that adds this file timestamps it. `scripts/mac-atlas-v8in.sh` copies the rule between the markers below into the local ledger (`kit run init`, which hashes it) before its first inference, and refuses to run if the ledger holds a different rule.

## Question

Does v8in (YoussefMoNader/ink-8um-v8in, revision `d89166b`) show ink on surfaces where `ink_9um` and the team's Hecate model, read by rodriguescarson, did not pass all four of his preregistered screens? One variable changes: the ink model. Surfaces, renders and depth order are those already published.

## Surfaces

All 81 meshes on PHerc0813 (75), PHerc0358 (3) and PHerc0826 (3) in [rodriguescarson/eligible-scroll-atlas](https://github.com/rodriguescarson/eligible-scroll-atlas) at commit `0c0566c`, from pscamillo's spiral fits. Surface volumes from the [atlas renders](https://huggingface.co/datasets/rodriguescarson/eligible-scroll-atlas-renders): 31 planes, plane 15 on the surface, team layer order (`--flip-normals`). v8in reads the central 24 planes. None was edited by hand. Five of them (PHerc0358 z11280_w020; PHerc0813 z5888_w020, z7696_w020, z12496_w060, z13088_w040) are the ones rodriguescarson holds back and sent to the team.

## Before the target

v8in must first pass on Mac MPS: `scripts/mac-w045.sh` CPU vs MPS crop check (`kit verify` pass), and its w045 AUC is recorded for scale.

## Readout rule

<!-- readout:start -->
A candidate is a region where, by eye, a v8in map shows stroke-like marks 2 to 4 mm tall arranged in at least two rows 2.5 to 8 mm apart, extending over at least 1 cm, in one depth direction and not the other; and the same marks appear when v8in is re-run on that mesh at a second, different stride. Marks that follow mesh edges, hole rims, onion-ring bands visible in the render, or render boundaries do not count. kit rowscore and any AUC are triage aids for choosing what to look at first and never decide. Every mesh with a row score in the top 10 in either direction is looked at, and so is every held-back mesh. Anything else is a null for that mesh.
<!-- readout:end -->

## What happens to the results

Maps stay on the user's machine (`$WORK/atlas`, outside the repository). A null is recorded with `kit run status ... null` and may be written up. A candidate is recorded as `candidate` and nothing about it is published or described until the prize is announced; see [`../WORKFLOW.md`](../WORKFLOW.md) section 3b.
