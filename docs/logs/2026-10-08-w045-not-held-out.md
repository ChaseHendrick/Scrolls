# w045 is not held out from `ink_9um` (2026-10-08)

**Question.** Since 7 October this repository has called PHerc0139 w045 "held out from `ink_9um`, v8in and d9v2" and used it as a generalization check. Is that right?

**Answer.** Only for v8in. `ink_9um` trained on the same papyrus surface through another scan, so for `ink_9um` and the readers fine-tuned from it, w045 is a cross-scan check on a training surface, not a held-out segment. No number changes; the wording around the numbers does.

## What the label dataset says (Sourced fact)

Source: the `ink_9um` label dataset README, [scrollprize/datasets, `ink_9um/README.md`](https://huggingface.co/buckets/scrollprize/datasets/tree/ink_9um), file updated 2026-08-14, read 2026-10-08. The [`ink_9um` model card](https://huggingface.co/scrollprize/ink_9um) points to it for "the per-segment source-volume tables" of its training data.

- The dataset has two label folders. `aligned-scrollprizeorg-21slices` holds 24 segments whose annotations were "transferred onto public 2.4 µm surface volumes, then pooled 4x in XYZ to ~9.6 µm". `native9-scrollprizeorg-21slices` holds 5 segments annotated "directly on native 9.362 µm PHerc. 0139 volumes": w035, w039, w040, w041 and w044.
- In the aligned table, annotation segment `pherc0139-w029` has source volume `20260126000000-w045_2026012619`, surface volume `2.399um-0.22m-78keV-volume-20260102150214.zarr`. That is the public segment our `w045` alias names (`kit/fetch.py`).
- The README warns that annotation names "do not track the public segment w-numbering". The public w045 appears there as w029, which is how the overlap was missed.
- The training config reads both folders, and the loader "finds `<segments_path>/<segment>/<segment>_inklabels.zarr` and friends on its own, so the layout here is exactly what a training run consumes". The model card's training data matches: PHerc0139 (9 aligned), Scroll 1667 (6), Paris 4 (8), PHerc0814 (1), plus the 5 native PHerc0139 segments.
- `pherc0139-w029` has no validation mask. The three online-validation segments are pherc0139-w016, pherc0814-46527 and pherc1667-w029; the last is a Scroll 1667 surface, not w045.

## What our w045 test uses

- Surface: `PHerc0139/segments/20260126000000-w045_2026012619/surface-volumes/9.362um-1.2m-113keV-volume-20250728140407.zarr`, the native 9.362 µm render of the same segment from a different scan (1.2 m, 113 keV, against 0.22 m, 78 keV for the 2.399 µm scan). It is not in the native-9 list.
- Labels: that segment's `ink-labels/2.399um-volume-20260102150214/20260918`, human labels drawn on the 2.399 µm render and mapped to the 9 µm grid by `kit auc` (`docs/results.json`, `segments.w045`).
- Interpretation: our test labels and `ink_9um`'s training labels for this surface come from the same annotation of the same 2.399 µm render. The 20260918 version postdates the dataset README, so it may include later revisions; we have not compared the two.

So, for `ink_9um`, w045 measures whether a reader trained on a surface's labels at about 9.6 µm (pooled from 2.399 µm) finds that ink again in a native 9.362 µm render from another scan. That is a cross-scan check, not generalization to unseen papyrus (Interpretation).

PHerc0841 is in neither dataset, so it remains the held-out test for every reader above.

## What relied on w045 being held out

- **README Novel finding 1** (v8in 0.738 against `ink_9um` 0.914 on the w045 crop). v8in never saw w045; `ink_9um` trained on it. The comparison favours `ink_9um` more than its caveat said, and it cannot show that v8in trails `ink_9um` on unseen papyrus. On PHerc0841, Bullo27 reports v8in at 0.837, 0.807 and 0.810 on the labels' bounding boxes of w00, ag896 and ag405 ([v8in-12gb](https://github.com/Bullo27/v8in-12gb), 2026-10-01, Community report), and this repository measured `ink_9um` seed 42 at 0.806, 0.659 and 0.784 on the 640 px crops. The windows differ, so these are not a head-to-head. The pending Phase 0 queue (v8in on the three PHerc0841 crops, `scripts/mac-phase0.sh`) is the fair comparison. The README now says this; whether to keep, reword or withdraw the finding is the owner's call.
- **README Novel finding 3** (9.5 % of w045's labelled ink reaches the w035 median). Reworded: w045 is a surface `ink_9um` trained on through another scan. The PHerc0841 figures are unaffected.
- **`docs/results.json` `held_out.w045`.** Corrected. Every w045 row keeps its number; the rows were always labelled w045, not "held out".
- **`kit gate`.** Unaffected: it ranks readers on the three PHerc0841 crops only (`kit/gate.py`, `SEGMENTS`).
- **d9v2 numbers.** The w045 d9v2 crop score (0.860) is a training-surface score, like `ink_9um`'s. The d9v2 fine-tune sweep selected checkpoints on PHerc0841 w00 and ag896, so its numbers and conclusion do not depend on w045.
- **Bullo27's w045 row scores** (73 to 148, and 148 for the 14-checkpoint average in `docs/compute.md`). Still Community reports, now read as scores on an `ink_9um` training surface.
- **Wording only, no behaviour change:** AGENTS glossary, `docs/AI-AGENTS.md` rule 6, `docs/mac.md`, `docs/scan-status.md`, `docs/compute.md`, `docs/plans/2026-10-07-training.md`, and comments or help text in `kit/fetch.py`, `kit/plan.py` (step 1b), `kit/auc.py`, `kit/cli.py`, `kit/rowscore.py` and `scripts/mac-w045.sh`. `tests/test_repo.py` now fails if a live file calls w045 held out from `ink_9um` again.
- **Left as written:** dated logs (2026-10-07 community scan, novel checks, w035 CPU and lane C), `docs/evidence/` and `scripts/experiments/`. They are records of what was believed then, and some are hashed in provenance files. This log supersedes their held-out wording for `ink_9um`. The handoff's 7 October follow-up notes already called w045 "a training-scroll pipeline control, not an independent-scroll result".

## Other surfaces in `ink_9um`'s training set

From the same table, the public PHerc0139 segments `ink_9um` trained on are w029 (as pherc0139-w016), w030 (as w017), w035, w039, w040, w041, w043, w044 and w045 (as w029). Anyone choosing a "held-out" PHerc0139 segment should check this list first, and match by source volume, not by w-number.
