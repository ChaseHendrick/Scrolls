## Shared direction

Refined editorial 3D scientific illustration with soft ivory light from the upper left and restrained copper rim light. Charcoal `#171A1C`, warm ivory `#EEE7D9` and oxidized copper `#B77A54`; close three-quarter view, warm desaturated contrast, seamless charcoal background and no people.

The Scrolls composition follows a layered papyrus roll into an open surface mesh. AutoPipeline follows a single selected sheet from its curled geometry into a flattened grid. Both are conceptual: physical unrolling of fragile carbonized papyrus is not the method used by the Vesuvius Challenge.
