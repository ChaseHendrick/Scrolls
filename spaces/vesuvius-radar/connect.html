Connect your tool

Everything this page shows is open JSON, CSV and RSS over HTTPS, and the whole radar is also an MCP server, so a script, a spreadsheet, a feed reader or an agent can use it. No key, no login and no GPU quota: anyone can call it, logged in or not.

MCP server for agents

Item titles, summaries and links belong to their sources (arXiv, GitHub, Hugging Face, PyPI, Zenodo, Crossref, the Vesuvius Challenge blog, and news outlets found through Google News, Bing News and Hacker News); the radar's code is Apache 2.0 · Streamable HTTP, no session, JSON replies, no key. Nine tools: search_items (by words or by meaning), get_item, find_similar, briefing, overview, source_status, check_now, list_views, get_settings.
