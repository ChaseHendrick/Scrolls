Features from the Vesuvius Challenge's dinovol model for every voxel of the PHerc0841 9.366 um scan, computed and shared by Chase Hendrick.
The page explains what they are, lets you click a point of one slice and see every cube that looks like it, and gives tested commands to stream the data.
Data: the public bucket SharpMeow/dinovol-embeddings (CC BY-NC 4.0, like the scan). Page source: spaces/dinovol-embeddings in ChaseHendrick/Scrolls.
